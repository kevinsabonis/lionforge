# LFP Performance — Site Polish Ideas

## Visual direction

Keep the existing tactical performance-dossier look: deep navy, warm gold accents, concise type, and a research-reference tone. The goal is to make the experience feel like a polished brand front door rather than only a reference table.

## Enhancement list

1. **Use a small, intentional image system**
   - Introduce three to four consistent images: an abstract molecular texture, clean lab glassware, athletic recovery/training, and an abstract neural or metabolic treatment.
   - Use them sparingly as section backgrounds with a dark navy overlay so text remains easy to read.
   - Keep the color grade cohesive: dark, restrained, navy and gold.

2. **Create a branded landing page before the guide**
   - Lead with a concise hero such as: “Research Smarter. Perform Better.”
   - Give visitors two clear choices: **Explore the Guide** and **Use the Calculator**.
   - This creates a true front door for the LFP brand.

3. **Give each subject area a visual identity**
   - Healing & Regeneration: cellular repair or recovery image treatment.
   - Performance & Growth Hormone: strength and training image treatment.
   - Cognitive: abstract neural image treatment.
   - Metabolic / Weight Management: clean metabolic or molecular treatment.

4. **Add quick-start pathways below the hero**
   - New to peptides.
   - Build a recovery stack.
   - Understand reconstitution.
   - Use the calculator.
   - Present these as clear cards that guide a new visitor into the right area.

5. **Make the guide tables easier to absorb**
   - Add slightly more white space and softer row dividers on desktop.
   - On mobile, show each peptide as an expandable card instead of forcing users to scroll a wide table.

6. **Remove or reframe generic purchase actions**
   - Remove generic **BUY ↗** links where they are not necessary.
   - If a link remains useful, prefer neutral language such as **Research Notes** or **Learn More**.
   - This better matches the educational positioning.

7. **Add a compact trust statement near the top**
   - Suggested format: “Research reference only · Educational tools · Built for practical comparison.”
   - This reinforces the purpose without feeling overly legal or promotional.

8. **Add a human founder / mission section**
   - Briefly connect LFP to Lion Forge Peptides and the Texas-based husband-and-wife story.
   - Focus on practical research, personal learning, and helping people navigate products more thoughtfully.

## Recommended first phase

1. Build the branded LFP landing page.
2. Add the quick-start cards and two primary calls to action.
3. Establish the four-image visual system.
4. Refresh the guide’s mobile presentation and remove/reframe the generic purchase links.

## Desired outcome

LFP should feel like a credible, premium education-and-tools destination: easy for a first-time visitor to understand, visually connected to Lion Forge Peptides, and distinct from a simple peptide reference sheet.
