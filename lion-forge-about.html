Our Story

Why two people in Texas started a peptide company — and what we wish someone had told us first.

Lion Forge Peptides isn't a warehouse full of strangers. It's the two of us, working out of Texas, doing something we wish someone had already done for us.

Before this was a business, it was a problem we were trying to solve for ourselves. We went looking for legitimate research peptides and ran into exactly what a lot of people run into: suppliers who took the payment and disappeared, and suppliers who shipped something — just not what the label promised.

We got scammed more than once. We were sold substances we wouldn't trust today, because back then, we didn't know enough to ask the right questions.

We kept looking anyway. Eventually we found what we'd been chasing the whole time — the right compounds, sourced properly. What changed for us after that wasn't small.

Better recovery. More energy. A version of ourselves we hadn't felt in years.
It reawakened something — and that's not a word we use lightly.

That experience is the entire reason Lion Forge Peptides exists. We built the sourcing relationships we wish we'd had from the very start, so the next person doesn't have to get burned twice before finding something real. Every product we carry is something we'd hand to each other without a second thought — because we still do, every day.

We're not a faceless supplier hiding behind a storefront. We're two people in Texas who made the mistakes so you don't have to, and built this because we didn't want anyone else to have to learn it the hard way.
